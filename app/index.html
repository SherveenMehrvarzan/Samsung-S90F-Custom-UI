AirPlay

Your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. On the big screen.

  1. Use the same Wi-FiConnect your Apple device to the same network as this TV.
  2. Open Screen MirroringOn iPhone, iPad, or Mac, open Control Center and choose Screen Mirroring.
  3. Choose your Samsung TVIf a code appears on the TV, enter it on your Apple device.

AirPlay settings

To enable AirPlay, open Samsung Settings, then Connection → Apple AirPlay Settings.

Start sharing from your Apple device. The TV opens the incoming AirPlay session automatically.
